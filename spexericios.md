 18. SIMPLE PRESENT – EXERCÍCIOS
 1. Descubra o erro
 Todas as frases abaixo têm um erro no Simple Present. Encontre o erro e reescreva a frase corretamente.

 a) She works at a hospital. 
 b) They like pizza. 
 c) He studies English every day. 
 d) We go to school by bus. 
 e) My brother watches TV at night. 
 f) I like chocolate.

 2. Complete e descubra a regra
 Complete as frases com a forma correta do verbo entre parênteses.

 a) I Study English every day. (study) 
 b) She Studies English every day. (studies) 
 c) They go to school by bus. (go) 
 d) He goes to school by bus. (goes) 
 e) We watch TV at night. (watch) 
 f) My sister watches TV at night. (watches) 
 g) You Drink coffee in the morning. (drink) 
 h) My father drinks coffee in the morning. (drinks)

 4. Pergunte para descobrir

 Exemplo: you / work / on Saturdays? → Do you work on Saturdays?
